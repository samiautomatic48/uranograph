What a pound costs to mine

Each block is a mine. Its width is annual output, its height the cost the owner reports. Where the line of reactor need crosses the blocks is the cost of the last pound required.

The price of a pound.

Spot is what a pound of U₃O₈ costs for delivery now. Term is the base price utilities sign for deliveries years out. The marks along the bottom of the chart are the events that moved the market.

Converting and enriching it

Uranium is the product. Turning it into gas and enriching it are services, priced separately. All three have repriced since their lows.

What utilities still have to buy.

Reactor operators buy most of their uranium years ahead, under long contracts. The United States publishes how much its operators have locked in, how much is still open, what sits in inventory and where it was mined.
