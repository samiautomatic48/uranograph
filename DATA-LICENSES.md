# Data credits and licences

This site reports public data. Each file under `data/` carries its sources in the page's "Sources and method" section.

- `data/reactors.json`: compiled from IAEA PRIS and World Nuclear Association public pages. Coordinates derive from
  GeoNuclearData, which is published under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. This file is offered under the same
  licence: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- `data/production_by_country.json` and the Red Book ranges in `data/outlook.json`: OECD-NEA and IAEA,
  "Uranium: Resources, Production and Demand", CC BY 4.0. The latest year is the sum of the mines listed on this site.
- US tables in `data/outlook.json` and the `eia` series in `data/prices.json`: US Energy Information Administration,
  public domain.
- The `imf` series in `data/prices.json`: International Monetary Fund, Primary Commodity Price System. Non-commercial
  use with attribution.
- `data/mines.json`, `data/companies.json`, `data/filings.json`: compiled from company reports and regulatory filings.
- `data/news.json`: headlines belong to their publishers and link to the original. The one-line summaries are this
  site's own.
- `data/world.json`: Natural Earth land outlines.
- Drawings in `js/05-art.js` and `img/art/`: original work made for this site.

Nothing here is investment advice.
