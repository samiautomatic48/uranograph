Uranium supply and demand to 2040

Reactors need about 179 million lb U₃O₈ (68,988 tU) in 2026, and mines are modelled to produce 188 million lb U₃O₈ (72,444 tU). The tables follow both to 2040, first on the owners' own schedules, then with new mines late and short.

The three cases are settings on the interactive figure, applied to restarts and new mines. They are tests of the schedule, not forecasts. Secondary supply is counted in all three.

What the tables assume

  • Reactor need counts running units, units under construction and planned units on their stated dates. A first core is booked the year before a unit connects.
  • Operating mines follow reported production, then guidance or last output. This column includes mines that are not listed one by one.
  • In the first table, restarts and new mines arrive on their owners' dates and deliver their full plan. That is the most favourable case. The second table delays them and cuts what they deliver.
  • Proposed mines are left out.
  • Secondary supply (inventories, underfeeding, recycling) is an assumption, not a measurement.
  • Nothing here is a price forecast.
